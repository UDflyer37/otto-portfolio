01 / About

I build systems that find what shouldn't be there, in the air or on the wire.

Research and Development Engineer and Army Cyber Officer based in Sunbury, Ohio. My work sits where applied AI, RF sensing, and cyber operations meet.

I lead development and commercialization of a passive bi-static radar capability that uses LEO satellite infrastructure as illuminators of opportunity for counter-UAS and wider airborne-object detection, and I deliver hands-on engineering leadership across classified cyber and mission-focused R&D programs.

I still wear the uniform part time as a Captain in the Ohio Army National Guard, where I help lead a defensive cyber team, build training infrastructure, and ran incident response as a joint force team lead during the Ohio Cyber Guardian 2026 exercise. Before that I spent two and a half years at Fort Drum as a Brigade Cyber and Electromagnetic Activities Officer, a Data Science Team Lead, and an Electronic Warfare Commander.

Born and raised in the Dayton, Ohio region, where my wife and I met as high school sweethearts and attended the University of Dayton together. We now live in Sunbury with our daughter and a very enthusiastic Aussiedoodle. Outside of work I mentor youth in cyber through volunteer programs. The fastest way to grow this field is to hand someone their first terminal.
